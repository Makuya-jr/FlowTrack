# FlowTrack

**Report water problems. Track every fix. Hold the responsible people accountable.**

FlowTrack is a water service monitoring system. Residents report water problems in their area, get early warnings, and can see in public whether, and how fast, those problems get fixed.

## The problem

- Water outages and leaks happen again and again in many South African cities.
- Large amounts of treated water are lost through leaking pipes before they reach homes. In Tshwane, where this project started, about a third of the water is lost this way.
- Residents report problems, but there is no public record of **if** or **when** they get fixed.

## What the system will do

- [ ] Report a problem (leak, burst pipe, no water, low pressure) with a photo and location
- [ ] Track each report: Reported → In progress → Fixed
- [ ] Public dashboard: open problems and average fix time per area
- [ ] Early alerts for planned maintenance and low-pressure warnings
- [ ] SMS alerts for people without smartphones

## Planned features

- [ ] **Location detection**: FlowTrack detects where you are and opens straight to your area's outages, leaks and fix times
- [ ] Pressure sensor integration (IoT) for automatic early warnings
